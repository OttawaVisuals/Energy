How a retrofit cost is calculated

These are incremental costs — the extra spend over the business-as-usual (BAU) choice a homeowner would have made anyway, not the full invoice. Insulation and air sealing have no BAU equivalent, so incremental = full cost. Windows are the efficient − standard premium. An ASHP is netted against the home's own pre-audit heating system (like-for-like — an oil boiler is credited as an oil boiler, not a generic gas furnace; gas furnace is now only the fallback when REMDB has no row at all for that fuel/type) plus the A/C it replaces (and can come out negative). See the review page for the per-record like-for-like vs. assumed-default breakdown. Read each measure left to right: named ERS columns → a derivation into what the cost regression needs → the priced rows → a dollar figure. Amber chips are assumptions — things ERS does not record and we had to choose. Hover any chip for the reasoning. All figures are 2023 USD, unadjusted for Canadian labour or exchange.
Scope: single-dwelling homes only. Multi-dwelling buildings (apartments, duplexes, triplexes — 311 of the paired PEI records) are excluded from costing: their ERS record describes the whole building, so REMDB's single-family per-ft²/per-window curves do not apply.
