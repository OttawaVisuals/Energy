How this works (plain language)

Each question is built by a script from the same data tables that power the other pages on this site. Nothing is typed in by hand, so the answer you see is the number in the table.

  • Compare, rank, find its place: you sort real numbers, such as how much energy each province's homes use.
  • Guess the gap: pick roughly how many times bigger one is than the other.
  • Guess a share: drag a slider to estimate a percentage. You are right if you land within the stated margin, which is wider on Easy and tighter on Hard.
  • Then vs now: which one rose more, or fell less, between two years.

Easy, medium and hard mean how far apart the numbers are: close calls are harder. It is a rule of thumb, not something measured from players.

Modelled or measured? Each question carries a badge. Modelled numbers are NRCan's estimates of how energy is used; measured numbers are counts, like homes started. Neither is a meter reading for your house.

Your best score is kept in your browser only. Nothing is sent anywhere.
